## Facing Ratz (2843657420) — 551 subs
An imitation of the classic Unreal Tournament map Facing Worlds created by someone who never played the original. Supports Deathmatch, TDM and Capture the Flag.

Version 1.0, may update as I familiarize myself more with the editor and tweak for gameplay balance. I have noticed a couple little bugs that currently are more of a novelty than a detriment but may be removed if I get around to it.

To-do list: fix top tower lighting, improve texturing, make more accurate to UT map/improve gameplay flow.
Potential changes: may make this a "mini" version, section between the towers is shorter than expected considering Ratz movement speed.
